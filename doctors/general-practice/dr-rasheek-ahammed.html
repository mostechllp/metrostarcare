About Dr. Rasheek Ahammed

Dr. Rasheek Ahammed is a medical professional with over 8 years of clinical experience. He completed his MBBS from Calicut Medical College and his DNB in Emergency Medicine from Kokilaben Dhirubhai Ambani Hospital, Mumbai — one of India's premier tertiary-care institutions.

He is experienced in managing a wide range of medical emergencies, acute illnesses, and lifestyle-related diseases, with a strong focus on timely diagnosis, stabilization, and comprehensive patient care.

Fluent in English, Hindi, and Malayalam, Dr. Rasheek ensures clear communication and a comfortable healthcare experience for patients from diverse backgrounds.

At Metro Starcare, he brings a calm, methodical approach — a critical asset in emergency and acute care settings — helping patients feel reassured while receiving evidence-based treatment.
