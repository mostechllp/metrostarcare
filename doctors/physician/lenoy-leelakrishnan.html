About Dr. Lenoy Leelakrishnan Remadevi

Dr. Lenoy Leelakrishnan Remadevi is a highly experienced General Physician and Diabetologist with over 19 years of clinical experience across the UAE, Oman, India, and the UK. He is dedicated to providing comprehensive, patient-centered care with a focus on evidence-based medicine and compassionate treatment.

Dr. Lenoy holds an MD (MBBS equivalent) from Kuban State Medical University, Russia, an MRes in Neuroscience from Aston University, UK, and a Fellowship in Diabetology from the Royal Liverpool Academy & Medvarsity, UK. He is also ACLS and BLS certified by the American Heart Association, ensuring he is equipped to handle complex medical emergencies with the highest standard of care.

With extensive expertise in General Medicine, Diabetology, ICU/CCU management, Emergency Medicine, and Chronic Disease Management, Dr. Lenoy has a proven track record of managing acute and chronic conditions with precision and empathy. His experience in critical care and emergency settings across multiple countries gives him a unique perspective on delivering high-quality healthcare under diverse circumstances.

Dr. Lenoy is particularly passionate about diabetes management and preventive care, empowering patients with the knowledge and tools they need to manage their conditions effectively and improve their quality of life. He believes in a holistic approach to health, addressing not just the symptoms but the underlying causes of disease.

Fluent in English, Malayalam, Hindi, Tamil, and Russian, Dr. Lenoy is able to connect with a wide range of patients, ensuring clear communication and a comfortable healthcare experience. His commitment to continuous learning and professional development keeps him at the forefront of medical advancements, benefiting every patient he sees.
