About Dr. Deepu Sunitha Soman

Dr. Deepu Sunitha Soman is an Orthopaedic Surgeon with more than three years of clinical experience in orthopaedic trauma, joint replacement, arthroscopy, sports injuries and outpatient orthopaedic care. She holds a DNB in Orthopaedics, a Diploma in Orthopaedics and MBBS, and has worked as a Consultant and Junior Consultant in established multi-speciality hospitals in Kerala, India.

Her clinical experience includes fracture management, knee and hip replacement procedures, knee and shoulder arthroscopy, periarticular injuries and rehabilitation-focused care.

She has completed additional training in arthroplasty and arthroscopy and has attended advanced courses in foot and ankle surgery, shoulder surgery, robotic knee arthroplasty and trauma care.

She is registered with the Travancore-Cochin Medical Council (TCMC) and is a life member of the Kerala Orthopaedic Association and the Indian Medical Association. Her professional approach emphasizes evidence-based treatment, patient safety, teamwork and functional recovery.

At Metro Starcare, Dr. Deepu brings a patient-first philosophy — diagnosing with precision, communicating clearly, and tailoring treatment plans to each individual's recovery goals.
