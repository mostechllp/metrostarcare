About Dr. Elham Ahmed Elgabaly Moustafa

Dr. Elham Ahmed Elgabaly Moustafa is a Consultant Radiologist with over 27 years of experience in diagnostic and interventional radiology. She holds an MD (PhD) in Radiodiagnosis and has built a distinguished career spanning clinical practice, medical education, and research.

She has extensive expertise in women's imaging, breast imaging and interventions, obstetric and gynecological ultrasound, pediatric radiology, and general ultrasound. Her experience in high-volume diagnostic centers has made her a trusted authority in complex imaging cases.

She has also contributed significantly to medical education, research, and clinical training in the UAE, mentoring the next generation of radiologists and shaping best practices in diagnostic imaging.

Fluent in Arabic and English, she brings world-class imaging expertise to Metro Starcare and ensures precise, patient-centered diagnostic care for every patient she encounters.
