About Dr. Fasil Variyathodi

Dr. Fasil Variyathodi is a highly experienced and trusted General Dentist in Sharjah, practicing at Metro Starcare Medical Centre, Rolla. He holds a B.D.S (Bachelor of Dental Surgery) from MES Dental College, Kerala, and is a proud UAE Golden Visa holder — a distinction awarded to outstanding professionals who contribute significantly to the UAE's healthcare landscape.

With over 13 years of clinical experience, Dr. Fasil has built a strong reputation in Sharjah for diagnosing and treating a wide range of oral diseases, creating personalized treatment plans, and performing complex dental procedures with precision and care. He is consistently recognized for maintaining high patient satisfaction scores and for improving long-term oral health outcomes for patients across the UAE.

He specializes in single-sitting root canal treatments, wisdom tooth extraction, and smile makeovers — three areas where surgical precision meets aesthetic sensibility. His approach combines modern dental technology with a genuinely patient-first mindset, ensuring that every visit is comfortable, clearly explained, and reassuring for patients of all ages.

Dr. Fasil is currently practicing at Mampilly Medical Centre (UAE) since 2019 and previously served at City Dental Clinic from 2013 to 2019. He completed his internship at MES Dental College, Kerala in 2012–2013. His career reflects a deep commitment to continuous learning and to delivering world-class dental care to the Sharjah community.

Whether you need a routine check-up, an emergency wisdom tooth extraction, a same-day root canal, or a complete smile transformation, Dr. Fasil offers reliable, affordable, and high-quality dental care at Metro Starcare Medical Centre — one of the most trusted multi-specialty clinics in Rolla, Sharjah.
