Ophthalmology Services in Sharjah

Good vision is essential for everyday activities, communication, learning, and maintaining independence. Eye problems can develop at any stage of life, and some conditions may progress without noticeable symptoms in the early stages. Regular eye examinations and timely medical attention can help identify vision concerns and support better long-term eye health.

We have an ophthalmology department at Metro Starcare that offers professional eye services to all people of different ages. We prioritize proper evaluation, effective communication, and treatment tailored to the needs of each individual.

Comprehensive Ophthalmology Care

Our ophthalmology services have been put in place to help individuals with all kinds of problems associated with their eyes and vision. This can include an eye examination, assessment of vision, testing for some common eye diseases, and follow-up care.

One may consider getting his eyes examined if he/she experiences blurry vision, strain in the eyes, too much watering, reddening of the eyes, irritation, headaches, and problems in seeing clearly. Eye examinations are also helpful for those people who do not experience any symptoms at present.

Common Eye Conditions

Eye health can be affected by age, lifestyle, genetics, existing health conditions, and environmental factors. Common concerns may include refractive errors, dry eyes, cataracts, glaucoma, eye infections, allergies, and age-related changes in vision.

Vision screening is also good for children. The detection of any problems that might affect reading, learning or everyday tasks can be achieved through early identification of any vision problems. The following symptoms are indicative that your child needs an eye examination:

  • Persistent squinting
  • Sitting too close to the television screen
  • Having frequent headaches

When to See an Eye Specialist

Changes in vision should not always be ignored. Persistent blurred vision, eye discomfort, unusual redness, sensitivity to light, or other ongoing symptoms may require professional assessment. Sudden changes in vision or significant eye pain require prompt medical attention.

An eye doctor will be able to assess your symptoms, determine the cause, interpret the results, and suggest appropriate treatment. Listening to professional advice and going for follow-up visits may help maintain your eye health.

Patient-Centered Care at Metro Starcare

At Metro Starcare, we aim to provide a comfortable and supportive healthcare environment where patients can discuss their concerns openly. Our team focuses on understanding individual needs and providing clear information about examination findings, treatment options, and recommended follow-up.

However, we are of the opinion that eye care is not only about treating eye disorders. Good lifestyles, correct screen time, prevention of excessive sun exposure, and periodic check-ups by an expert in this field may help maintain good vision.
