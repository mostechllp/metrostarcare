Physiotherapy Services at Metro Starcare

Physiotherapy plays a vital role in restoring movement, reducing pain, and improving overall quality of life. At Metro Starcare Medical Centre, our Physiotherapy Department provides evidence-based physical therapy and rehabilitation services designed to help patients recover from injuries, manage pain effectively, and enhance mobility for a healthier and more active lifestyle. As highlighted in the service section, the department focuses on injury recovery, pain management, and mobility improvement, ensuring personalized treatment plans for every patient.

The highly skilled physiotherapists analyze the patient's health condition thoroughly and devise specialized treatment programs according to their needs. If you are suffering from an injury due to sports, surgery, muscle ache, joint problems, backache, neck pain, or any other mobility disorder, then physiotherapy can help you regain your lost energy, flexibility, and mobility. Instead of just alleviating the symptoms of pain, it aims at curing the actual problem causing the pain.

Moreover, physiotherapy has gained popularity for making people regain their freedom and confidence in performing regular tasks. In Metro Starcare, treatments can involve physical exercises, manual treatments, postural correction, stretching, mobility, and rehabilitation exercises designed to help each person recover.

The department offers evidence-based treatments, which means that each patient will get the latest clinical and rehabilitative treatment techniques. Patients are provided with a safe atmosphere for their recovery with continuous monitoring and evaluation of their progress.

Frequently Asked Questions

What conditions does Mrs. Hanitta Thimothy treat through physiotherapy?

Mrs. Hanitta Thimothy provides physiotherapy care for a wide range of musculoskeletal and rehabilitation conditions, including back pain, neck pain, joint pain, sports injuries, muscle strains, post-surgical rehabilitation, posture-related problems, and mobility limitations. Treatment plans are tailored to each patient’s condition and recovery goals.

When should I consult a physiotherapist?

You should consult a physiotherapist if you experience persistent pain, stiffness, reduced mobility, sports injuries, muscle weakness, balance issues, or difficulty performing daily activities. Early physiotherapy intervention can help reduce pain, improve movement, and prevent further complications.

Does Mrs. Hanitta Thimothy provide rehabilitation after surgery or injury?

Yes. Mrs. Hanitta Thimothy offers rehabilitation programs for patients recovering from orthopedic surgeries, fractures, ligament injuries, joint replacements, and other musculoskeletal conditions. Rehabilitation focuses on restoring strength, flexibility, mobility, and functional independence.

How many physiotherapy sessions will I need?

The number of physiotherapy sessions depends on your diagnosis, severity of the condition, overall health, and treatment goals. Some patients experience improvement within a few sessions, while others with chronic conditions or post-surgical recovery may require a longer rehabilitation plan.

What can I expect during my first physiotherapy consultation?

During your first visit, Mrs. Hanitta Thimothy will review your medical history, discuss your symptoms, assess your movement and physical function, and identify the underlying cause of your condition. Based on this assessment, a personalized treatment plan will be created to support your recovery.
