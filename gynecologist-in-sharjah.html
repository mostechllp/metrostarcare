Frequently Asked Questions

1. What Gynecology & Women's Health services are available at Metro Starcare Hospital?

Metro Starcare Hospital provides comprehensive reproductive and advanced gynecological care led by Dr. Sandhya Subhashchandran with over 22 years of experience.

Services include:

  • Annual gynecological examinations
  • PAP smear screening
  • Wellness screening
  • High-risk pregnancy care
  • Fertility consultation
  • Laparoscopic surgery
  • Traditional gynecological surgery
  • Cosmetic gynecology
2. What can I expect during an annual gynecology examination?

Patients receive:

  • Complete medical history review
  • Menstrual health discussion
  • Physical examination
  • Breast examination
  • Pelvic examination (if required)
  • PAP smear or HPV screening
  • Family planning consultation
  • Menopause and hormonal health guidance
3. At what age should a young woman first visit a gynecologist?

The first gynecology visit is generally recommended between 13–15 years of age.

Topics include:

  • Puberty
  • Menstrual health
  • HPV vaccination
  • Reproductive health
  • Preventive care
4. What conditions are treated?

Common conditions include:

  • PCOS
  • Irregular periods
  • Painful menstruation
  • Pregnancy complications
  • High-risk pregnancy
  • Preeclampsia
  • Fibroids
  • Ovarian cysts
  • Endometriosis
  • Pelvic organ prolapse
  • Infertility
5. Does Metro Starcare provide emergency gynecological consultation?

Yes.

Emergency consultation is available for:

  • Severe pelvic pain
  • Heavy abnormal bleeding
  • Pregnancy emergencies
  • High-risk pregnancy complications

Patients are treated by Dr. Sandhya Subhashchandran and the dedicated gynecology team.
