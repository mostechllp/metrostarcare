Dermatologist in Rolla

Whilst our skin is there to protect us, many factors can affect our skin. These include; the sun, environmental pollution and even our hormone levels and allergy status. Alongside this our lifestyle can also influence our skin. Occasionally, a seemingly small issue such as eczema or long lasting itchiness, can need medical assessment and intervention should it fail to resolve.

When Does Your Skin Need Medical Attention?

Whilst not all skin related issues are complex, it is important that people do not disregard persistent and atypical skin conditions. Skin discolouration, chronic dryness and rash as well as abrupt hair loss can all require further assessment.

People are urged to seek specialist advice for ongoing and uncomfortable skin issues. This allows us to investigate likely causes and then suggest a relevant form of treatment, depending on an individual's situation.

Common Skin Problems We Address

Different skin conditions require different approaches to diagnosis and treatment. At Metro Starcare, our dermatology and cosmetology department provides medical support for a variety of skin-related concerns.

Our areas of care include:

  • Acne and Breakouts: Assessment of recurring pimples, inflamed acne, blackheads, and related skin concerns.
  • Dry and Sensitive Skin: Medical guidance for persistent dryness, irritation, itching, and skin sensitivity.
  • Uneven Skin Tone: Evaluation of sunspots, melasma, dark patches, and other pigmentation concerns.
  • Chronic Skin Conditions: Management guidance for eczema, psoriasis, and other long-term dermatological problems.
  • Infectious Skin Conditions: Diagnosis and treatment guidance for bacterial, fungal, and other skin infections.
  • Scalp and Hair Disorders: Assessment of dandruff, scalp irritation, excessive hair shedding, and hair thinning.
  • Nail Health Problems: Medical evaluation of brittle nails, fungal infections, and unusual nail changes.

We believe that identifying the underlying cause of a skin problem is important before deciding on suitable treatment.

Personalised Treatments for Different Skin Needs

People react to their environment, products and medical interventions in different ways. We analyse a patient’s skin type, issues and medical background to tailor their advice.

In addition to our dermatology and beauty care expertise we also offer specialist advice and treatment for issues such as scarring, uneven skin texture, discoloration and ageing. Again depending on the issue, we can suggest a combination of medical, beauty and cosmetic care.

We endeavour to advise and support patients so that they are able to make informed decisions and benefit from a good skin care regime in order to maintain good skin health.

Skin Care Support at Metro Starcare Medical Centre

We recognise that individuals can present with various skin issues. We will work with you to assess and advise on suitable dermatology treatment.

Should you wish to seek expert advice in relation to acne, skin inflammation, discoloration and further skin related issues then we can provide you with suitable information in order that you can make well-considered choices regarding your skin.
