Advanced Radiology & Diagnostic Imaging at Metro Starcare

Getting the right diagnosis requires a clear view into the body, and that's where the Radiology department comes in. At Metro Starcare Medical Center, we offer the latest imaging technology and a team of Radiologists who work in close cooperation with your physician to ensure an accurate diagnosis. Our goal is to free up your doctor's time by providing the correct one, so that he or she can quickly determine the best course of treatment.

Whether you come in for a broken ankle, a routine pregnancy check-up, or a diagnostic procedure recommended by your doctor, you can count on the experienced team to provide the highest quality of care. We have a full range of equipment to meet all of your diagnostic imaging needs including x-rays, ultrasounds, CT scans, MRI, Mammography and Doppler studies. Our technologists are always focused on keeping you comfortable during your test, while our radiologists are committed to giving you a thorough and accurate diagnosis.

We know that waiting for scan results can be stressful, which is why we strive to return your results as soon as possible without compromising quality. After your scan, your results are immediately sent to your referring doctor so that they can be reviewed and discussed in a timely manner.

You will find our radiology unit in Metro Starcare Medical Centre at Rolla, Sharjah, an environment created to put you at ease. Our staff will make sure to spend enough time with you discussing what to expect before your test as well as answering any questions you may have. So if you have been referred by your physician for a scan or would like to talk to our radiology team first, we invite you to book a scan with us.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What imaging services do you offer?

We have a comprehensive range of equipment to meet all your diagnostic imaging needs:

  • X-ray
  • Ultrasound
  • CT Scan
  • MRI
  • Mammography
  • Doppler Studies
2. Do I need a doctor's referral for a scan?

In most cases, a referral from a physician is required for a scan, as it allows us to determine which test is needed depending on your condition. However, if you are unsure about what scan is needed for your case, our team will be able to advise you and sort it out for you.

3. Is it safe for pregnant women and children?

Ultrasounds are safe for both pregnant women and children since they do not involve exposure to radiation. X-ray scanners involve a low dose of exposure in children and pregnant women, and we have procedures to ensure that their health is prioritized. Children also require special scanning procedures.

4. How long does it take to get the scan results?

Report results can be provided immediately or within a short period of time depending on the case.

5. What happens during the appointment?
  • Check in and initial evaluation with our team of technologists regarding your scan referral or medical history.
  • The scanning procedure.
  • A review and summary of the following steps including when the results will be available.
6. Are your radiologists qualified?

Yes, our radiologists are all certified and qualified with experience in various fields of scanning as needed for the case.
