Pathology & Lab Services at Metro Starcare

Almost every diagnosis starts with a lab test, and that's exactly why our in-house Pathology and Lab department exists to give your doctor the answers fast, so you're not sitting around wondering what's actually going on with your health.

We run all kinds of tests right here on-site routine blood work, urine analysis, hormone panels, infection screening, allergy tests, you name it. Since it's all done in-house, there's no sending your samples off somewhere else and waiting days to hear back. Our lab team works hand in hand with your doctor, so whatever's been ordered gets processed quickly without any unnecessary back and forth.

Honestly, accuracy is everything in lab work get it wrong, and treatment can head in the wrong direction. So we don't cut corners. Our technicians and pathologists follow strict checks on every sample that comes through, and we keep our equipment up to date so you can actually trust what the report says. Whether you're in for a routine check-up, getting cleared for surgery, or keeping tabs on something like diabetes or thyroid levels, we treat it with the same care every time.

We also try to make the whole experience easy on you. Our staff are quick and gentle when it comes to blood draws, and we keep the process simple from the moment you walk in to the moment you get your results. Most routine reports are ready within a few hours not days and if something's urgent, we make sure it doesn't sit in a queue.

You'll find our lab inside Metro Starcare Medical Centre, Rolla, Sharjah. Come in with a referral from your doctor, or if you're not sure what test you need, just reach out and we'll help you figure it out.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What kind of tests can I get done at your lab?

A lot, honestly — blood tests, urine tests, hormone panels, infection and allergy screening, and more. If your doctor's ordered something specific, chances are we can run it right here.

2. Do I need a doctor’s referral for lab tests?

Yeah, it helps us know what we need to check for most tests. But if it's something like a general blood work up, you can usually just walk in.

3. How soon will I actually get my results?

Most routine ones are ready within a few hours. Some of the more detailed tests take a little longer but we always try to keep the wait short.

4. Do I need to fast before my blood test?

Depends on what you're getting done. Blood sugar or cholesterol tests? You have to fast beforehand, and we’ll always give you a heads-up so you’re prepared.

5. Are the results from your lab trustworthy?

Yes. We follow strict quality checks on everything that comes through, and our pathologists and technicians are fully qualified — so what you get back is something you can rely on.
