Dermatology & Cosmetology Services
at Metro Starcare

The health of your skin and hair is essential to your self-confidence and sense of well-being. In the Metro Starcare Medical Centre, our Department of Dermatology & Cosmetology offers you a wide range of treatments for skin, hair, and beautification that include dermatology and cosmetic procedures. As can be seen from the services offered section, this department specializes in providing specialized treatment for all types of skin problems and enhancing beauty.

Our expert dermatologists can diagnose and treat various problems related to the skin, hair, and nails, including acne, eczema, psoriasis, skin allergies, pigmentation problems, fungal infections, hair loss, dandruff, and more. Every patient gets an individual treatment program, which is designed according to his or her needs. The combination of modern technologies and our professionalism allows us to deliver the highest level of dermatological services.

Along with the medical branch of dermatology, the metro starcare practice offers cosmetic services which are targeted at enhancing the look of the skin and increasing self-confidence. Possible cosmetic procedures involve the treatment of skin rejuvenation, anti-aging, pigmentation, scarring, as well as any other non-surgical aesthetics procedures. All these treatments are done in order to make one's skin healthier and more youthful without changing its natural look.

In the dermatology and cosmetology division of Metro Starcare Medical Centre at Rolla, Sharjah, there is a supportive atmosphere of health care with modern facilities and expert professionals. There are highly professional medical staff members who try their best to give high-level health care services to their patients. If you want to treat your skin diseases or hair problems and want to improve your looks by applying cosmetics on your skin, then you can make an appointment with our dermatologist and visit.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What clinical skin conditions are treated at Metro Starcare Hospital?

Our Dermatology and Cosmetic Department offers comprehensive medical and surgical care for a wide range of concerns affecting the skin, hair, and nails. We specialize in diagnosing and managing common and complex conditions, including:

  • Acne and acne scarring
  • Psoriasis and eczema
  • Vitiligo
  • Complex pigmentation disorders
  • Skin allergies

Our specialists combine clinical diagnostics with advanced medical treatments to manage chronic issues and help patients achieve healthy skin.

2. What advanced anti-aging and skin rejuvenation therapies do you offer?

The Cosmetic Department provides a variety of advanced, non-surgical aesthetic treatments designed to restore volume, improve skin texture, and reduce signs of aging. Our treatments are tailored to each patient's goals and include:

  • Injectables: Botox and dermal fillers to smooth fine lines and restore facial volume.
  • Skin Resurfacing: Specialized chemical peels and advanced medical laser treatments.
  • Rejuvenation: Personalized anti-aging therapies to enhance skin tone and promote a youthful, radiant glow.
3. Does the department offer specialized solutions for hair loss?

Yes. We provide dedicated diagnostic evaluations for various types of hair loss and thinning. The department offers advanced non-surgical restoration treatments, including Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) therapy, which utilizes the body's natural growth factors to stimulate hair follicles, promote growth, and restore hair density.

4. Do you provide pediatric dermatological care for infants and children?

Yes, our department is fully equipped to care for our youngest patients. We offer specialized pediatric dermatological care designed to diagnose and safely treat delicate skin, hair, and nail conditions unique to infants, toddlers, and adolescents. Our team ensures a gentle, reassuring environment tailored to children's specific medical needs.

5. What should I expect during my first cosmetic or skin consultation?

Our department operates on a patient-first philosophy focused on clinical education and guidance. During your initial consultation, you can expect:

  1. A thorough physical evaluation of your skin, hair, or nails to understand how they relate to your overall well-being.
  2. An in-depth discussion regarding your medical history, current concerns, and aesthetic goals.
  3. A customized, step-by-step treatment plan designed to safely and effectively guide you through your skin health journey.
6. Are the dermatologists in the department fully certified and licensed?

Yes. All medical professionals in the Dermatology and Cosmetic Department at Metro Starcare Hospital hold valid licenses from the Ministry of Health (MOH) UAE. Our department is staffed by highly qualified specialists with extensive training in medical, surgical, and cosmetic dermatology, alongside active contributions to international medical research.
