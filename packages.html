Comprehensive Preventive Health Checkups in Sharjah

At Metro Starcare Medical Centre, Rolla, Sharjah, we believe early detection is key to maintaining lifelong wellness. Our tailored health packages are designed by experienced medical specialists to assess vital organ functions, detect silent health risks, and provide actionable health advice.

General & Cardiac Wellness

Evaluate lipid profiles, blood pressure, blood glucose, ECG, and Troponin markers to protect your cardiovascular system and manage metabolic risks.

Comprehensive Laboratory Tests

Detailed screening of Complete Blood Count (CBC), Vitamin D3, Vitamin B12, Thyroid profiles (TSH/T3/T4), Liver enzymes, and Renal parameters.

Women's & Specialized Care

Dedicated hormone checkups (FSH, LH, Prolactin), Gynecological consultations, pelvic ultrasounds, and aesthetic skin & hair treatments.
