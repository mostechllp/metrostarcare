Gynecologist in Al Khan

Many women ignore their health in order to manage work and home commitments and/or are unsure regarding how to interpret their bodies. Women should recognise and pay attention to alterations in their periods, new pains and hormonal changes. Awareness of these issues and appropriate professional guidance can then allow for improved self health management. We actively advise women to value and look after their health by attending medical appointments and undergoing preventative healthcare.

When Should You Consult a Gynecology Specialist?

Whilst not all health issues are debilitating, there may be subtle signs and symptoms that suggest someone requires medical intervention. Seeking professional advice enables health care professionals to assess and identify if additional investigation or intervention is required.

You should seek medical advice if you:

  • Notice irregular and/or heavy periods
  • Are continually suffering from pelvic/abdominal pain
  • Have abnormal/vaginal soreness
  • Experience intermenstrual bleeding
  • Are having difficulty in becoming pregnant
  • Are experiencing hormone related symptoms
  • Have period related issues which are impacting on your life

We feel that it is important to raise such issues at an early stage so that people can receive a prompt and suitable diagnosis and intervention.

Understanding Common Conditions Affecting Women's Health

Women can suffer from a number of issues which need to be assessed by healthcare professionals.

  • Polycystic ovary syndrome – can affect a woman’s hormones, and so in turn, her periods and fertility. Assessment is used to advise on how best to manage this issue.
  • Endometriosis – can result in painful periods, as well as pelvic pain and infertility. Assessment is then used to advise on the best way to manage this issue.
  • Uterus (womb) fibroids – are benign tumours in the womb. They can lead to heavy periods and/or pelvic pressure or pain.
  • Menstrual issues – women can experience irregular, long or very painful periods. Women can benefit from assessment for these issues.

By understanding these issues, we can then make informed decisions and access suitable healthcare where necessary.

Pregnancy Planning and Women's Wellness

As well as physically preparing for pregnancy, it is also important for women to be aware of their health and any current medical issues. We advise that women attend pre-conception appointments so that we can assess a woman’s health and offer appropriate advice.

Antenatal care is used to assess both mother and foetus’ health. Our gynaecology service includes family planning, assessment of fertility and menopause related issues together with general preventative women’s health care.

Take Care of Your Health with the Right Medical Support

Good health is something that should be looked after throughout your life. Advice can be sought for issues such as periods, for those wishing to conceive and for people wanting to undertake screening.
