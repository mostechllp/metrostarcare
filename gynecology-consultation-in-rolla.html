Gynecology Consultation in Rolla

At different stages of a woman’s lifetime her body will experience a number of different changes. Women often encounter new and differing health requirements at different times. For example, within a woman’s reproductive years there can be fluctuation in her period and as she reaches menopause, hormone levels can change. Consulting a healthcare professional about any concerns can enable women to further educate themselves on their bodies and implement suitable actions to improve their health.

Understanding Changes in Your Menstrual Cycle

Women can gain much information regarding their reproductive health. Altered cycle duration and excessive bleeding, amenorrhoea (absence of periods) and intense period pain could all suggest hormone related or other gynaecological issues.

Common Women's Health Concerns That Need Attention

There are numerous gynaecological health issues which may impact a woman. This may involve PCOS and also ovarian and womb cysts which can in themselves affect a woman and provoke symptoms including, for example, pain and abnormal periods.

Furthermore, women can acquire vaginal infections and produce abnormal discharge. Women should research further into these areas if they experience any of the following; itchiness, pain and tenderness so that suitable resources are available to recognise and manage the problem.

What to Expect During a Gynecology Consultation

Most consultations will include an assessment of your symptoms and also information regarding your period and previous medical history. Doctors may suggest that you undergo further assessment (such as physical, laboratory and scanning methods) depending on your presenting issues.

Depending on a person’s symptoms, age and health needs, different assessments may be advised. Being able to discuss your issues and to ask questions can aid your understanding of both the consultation and the care that can be provided.

Pregnancy Planning and Hormonal Health

Pre-conception appointments can be useful for women. These provide a health check and a chance to explore an individual’s lifestyle and also ascertain which investigations may be suitable.

Fluctuating hormone levels can also influence a woman’s period, fertility and health. Women can consult healthcare professionals if they are concerned about their periods, weight and menopause related symptoms in order to investigate and advise on how best to address these issues.

Make Time for Your Women's Health

As well as acting on information relating to your sexual and reproductive health when problems occur, it is also beneficial to educate yourself on these areas and to attend suitable screening appointments. It is key that you are able to talk through any worries or issues you have with a healthcare professional in order to make well-informed decisions.
