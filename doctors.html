Comprehensive Specialties

Frequently Asked Questions (Doctor Consultations)

How do I book a doctor's consultation at Metro Starcare?

You can click on the "Book" button next to any doctor's profile to open our online booking form, or call us directly at +971 52 841 5113.

Do your doctors accept major health insurance plans in UAE?

Yes, Metro Starcare Medical Centre accepts leading health insurance providers including Oman Insurance, Nextcare, NAS, Neuron, FMC, MetLife, NGI, AXA/GIG, and Al Madallah.

Are walk-in doctor consultations available?

Yes! Walk-in consultations are available daily from 06:30 AM to 11:00 PM for general practice and urgent health concerns.
