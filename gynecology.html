Our Core Gynecology Services

We provide a full range of preventative, diagnostic, and therapeutic services to manage every aspect of women's reproductive health:

  • Routine Preventive Screenings: Regular routine wellness visits, Pap smears Test to screen for cervical cancer, pelvic exams and clinical breast exams are important for early detection and continued health throughout a woman's life.
  • Pre-Conception & Early Pregnancy Care: Preparing for motherhood is an exciting step. Our team offers pre-pregnancy counseling, genetic screening advice, and early-stage prenatal care to ensure a healthy start for you and your baby.
  • Menopause & Perimenopause Management: Aging is a natural, beautiful transition. We help you manage symptoms such as hot flashes, mood shifts, bone density loss, and metabolic changes through tailored lifestyle and medical strategies.
  • Advanced Gynecological Diagnostics: Our center is equipped with the latest ultrasound imaging and diagnostic equipment to assess ovarian cysts, uterine fibroids and pelvic pain with precision and speed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Gynecology & Women's Health services are available at Metro StarCare Hospital?

Metro StarCare Hospital provides a full spectrum of comprehensive reproductive and advanced gynecological care led by Dr. Fareeda Mammu. With over 22 years of specialized experience, Dr. Fareeda offers personalized consultations and adaptive treatment plans spanning routine screenings to complex medical interventions. Available services include:

  • Routine Preventive Care: Annual gynecological exams, PAP smears, and wellness screenings.
  • Specialized Maternity & Fertility Care: High-risk pregnancy management and personalized infertility treatments.
  • Advanced Surgical Interventions: Complex gynecological surgeries utilizing both traditional and minimally invasive laparoscopic techniques.
  • Cosmetic Gynecology: Targeted procedures designed to restore comfort, function, and pelvic confidence.
What can I expect during an annual gynecology exam with Dr. Fareeda Mammu?

During an annual well-woman exam at Metro StarCare Hospital, patients receive a thorough, patient-centered health assessment. Dr. Fareeda focuses on a collaborative approach, ensuring you feel comfortable and informed. The exam typically includes:

  • Integrated Health Assessment: A detailed discussion of your medical history, menstrual cycle, and any specific reproductive health symptoms or goals.
  • Physical Screenings: Routine vital checks, a clinical breast examination, and a pelvic exam if clinically indicated based on your age and health history.
  • Preventive Diagnostics: Age-appropriate screenings, such as Pap smears or HPV testing, to detect cellular changes early.
  • Personalized Consultation: An open opportunity to discuss family planning, contraceptive management, hormonal balance, or menopausal transitions.
At what age should a young woman start seeing a gynecologist?

According to leading global healthcare standards (such as the ACOG), young women should schedule their first gynecological visit between the ages of 13 and 15.

Dr. Fareeda Mammu focuses on empowering young women with accurate medical knowledge, providing a safe, confidential space to discuss menstrual cycles, development, pelvic health, and HPV vaccinations. Establishing this relationship early builds a foundation for lifelong reproductive wellness.

What are the common conditions treated in obstetrics and gynecology at Metro StarCare Hospital?

Dr. Fareeda Mammu utilizes her advanced training and MRCOG (UK) credentials to diagnose and manage a wide array of reproductive health issues. Common conditions treated include:

  • Menstrual & Hormonal Disorders: Irregular bleeding, painful periods (dysmenorrhea), and Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS).
  • Pregnancy Complications: Management of gestational conditions, preeclampsia, and high-risk pregnancies requiring multidisciplinary care.
  • Structural & Pelvic Conditions: Fibroids, ovarian cysts, endometriosis, and pelvic organ prolapse.
  • Reproductive Challenges: Evaluative testing and tailored treatment paths for couples experiencing infertility.
Does Metro StarCare Hospital offer emergency or after-hours gynecological consulting?

Sure. Our hospital has been well-equipped to handle any urgent cases involving gynecology and even obstetrics. In cases where there is an emergency involving serious complications regarding women's reproductive health like severe pelvic pain, excessive and abnormal vaginal bleeding, or high-risk pregnancies, we will provide professional assistance through our dedicated medical professionals together with Dr. Fareeda Mammu.
