About Dr. Pradeep Kumar Shetty

From the moment he stepped into the world of medicine, Dr. Pradeep Kumar Shetty knew that helping people see the world more clearly would be his life's work. With over 29 years of experience, he has dedicated his career to diagnosing and treating a wide range of eye diseases, transforming lives through advanced ophthalmic care.

Beyond surgical excellence, Dr. Shetty is deeply passionate about preventive eye care. He believes that early detection is key, especially in conditions like pediatric ophthalmology (strabismus, amblyopia) and oculoplastic procedures that enhance both vision and aesthetics. His experience also extends to eye trauma and emergency care, making him a trusted specialist in critical situations.

Dr. Shetty's approach is not just about treating eye conditions—it's about enhancing quality of life. With a commitment to cutting-edge technology and personalized care, he ensures that every patient receives tailored solutions to suit their needs.

Dr. Shetty's expertise spans across cataract surgery, glaucoma management, and medical retinal procedures, ensuring that each patient receives the best possible treatment. He specializes in phacoemulsification cataract surgery, a cutting-edge technique that restores vision with minimal discomfort and quick recovery. Patients suffering from glaucoma, diabetic retinopathy, macular degeneration, or dry eye syndrome trust him for his precise diagnosis and effective treatment plans.
