About Dr. Fareeda Mammu

Dr. Fareeda Mammu is a highly experienced Specialist Obstetrician & Gynaecologist with 22+ years of clinical experience in providing comprehensive women's healthcare. She is dedicated to delivering compassionate, evidence-based medical care for women at every stage of life—from adolescence and reproductive health to pregnancy, childbirth, and menopause.

She has extensive expertise in managing high-risk pregnancies, routine antenatal care, infertility evaluation, menstrual disorders, PCOS, recurrent pregnancy loss, and complex gynecological conditions. Dr. Fareeda believes in a patient-centered approach, ensuring every woman receives personalized care, accurate diagnosis, and treatment plans tailored to her individual health needs.

Her clinical experience also includes performing gynecological, vaginal, and abdominal surgeries, along with procedures such as IUCD insertion and removal, HSG, and IUI. She is committed to helping women achieve better reproductive health through preventive care, early diagnosis, and modern treatment options.

Known for her compassionate nature and clear communication, Dr. Fareeda creates a comfortable environment where patients can openly discuss their health concerns. She works closely with every patient to promote long-term wellness, safe motherhood, and improved quality of life.
