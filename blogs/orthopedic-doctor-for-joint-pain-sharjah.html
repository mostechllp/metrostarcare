Common Causes of Joint Pain & When to Consult an Orthopedic Doctor

31-07-2026

Joint pain is a common ailment experienced by all age groups. Although there might be some joint pain associated with routine work and minor injuries, chronic and severe joint pains can affect mobility and productivity as well as quality of life. Learning about the reasons for joint pain and when you should consult an orthopedic physician can help avoid further problems with joints.

Metro Starcare knows that correct diagnosis and treatment of the problem is essential to achieve joint health and live an active life. No matter where your pain comes from: a knee, shoulder, hip, ankle or any other joint, diagnosis is the most important step.

What Is Joint Pain?

Joint pains are the feelings of irritation, stiffness, swelling, and soreness in the places where two or more bones join together. Joint pain can be either mild pain or very severe pain depending on the degree of joint injury and could occur suddenly after an injury or develop slowly over time.

In most instances, joint pain may occur only for a short while and could heal itself with rest and some self-care. Still, chronic signs must not be neglected because they can be related to some orthopedic issue underlying the condition.

Common Causes of Joint Pain

1. Osteoarthritis

Osteoarthritis is one of the most frequent reasons for joint pain, particularly among elderly people who change to among 40+ aged peoples. It happens because the cushioning cartilage found in your joints wears away gradually.

2. Sports and Physical Injuries

Various types of accidents, injuries, and falls can have an impact on the joints, muscles, and ligaments in the affected area. A person suffering from a strain, sprain, or fracture can experience pain and swelling.

Ignoring an injury may increase the risk of long-term joint problems and chronic discomfort.

3. Bursitis

Bursae are sacs that contain fluid and serve as cushioning structures of the joints, reducing friction. Bursitis is an inflammatory condition of the sacs. It can occur in the shoulders, elbows, hips, and knees.

People who perform repetitive movements are at a higher risk of developing bursitis.

4. Gout

Gout is a form of arthritis that results from the accumulation of crystals of uric acid in the joints. The joints that get affected include the big toe, ankle, and knee joint.

Gout attacks typically occur suddenly and can cause severe pain, redness, and swelling.

Signs You Should Not Ignore

While occasional joint discomfort may not require immediate medical attention, certain symptoms should prompt a professional evaluation.

  • Persistent pain lasting several weeks
  • Joint swelling or redness
  • Difficulty walking or moving the joint
  • Limited range of motion
  • Pain that worsens over time
  • Joint instability or weakness
  • Pain following an injury or accident

When to Consult an Orthopedic Doctor in Sharjah

If joint pain begins affecting your daily activities, work performance, exercise routine, or overall comfort, it is time to consult an orthopedic doctor in sharjah. Our orthopedic specialists are trained to diagnose and treat conditions affecting bones, joints, ligaments, muscles, and tendons.

Orthopedic evaluation is a broad term that can include physical exams, imaging procedures like X-rays or MRI scans, as well as a patient’s medical history. Treatment could consist of drugs, physiotherapy, lifestyle changes, or even surgery.

How an Orthopedic Surgeon in Sharjah Can Help

Sometimes, a patient suffering from a complex joint problem or an injury needs surgery as a treatment method. The orthopedic specialist in Sharjah can tell if the surgery will be needed if conservative treatment does not work.

Modern techniques in orthopedics involve the restoration of joints’ natural functioning, pain reduction, and fast recovery. The type of treatment differs depending on the disease and may include joint surgery, ligament repair, fracture treatment, or surgery for joint replacement.
