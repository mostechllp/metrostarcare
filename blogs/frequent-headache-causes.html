Common Causes of Frequent Headaches and When to Seek Medical Advice

25-11-2026

Everyone experiences headaches at some point. They can be caused by tiredness, lack of sleep, or being very stressed. However, if we experience regular, severe or difficult to manage headaches, it would be best to understand what could be causing the headaches so that you can look after your health better. Knowing what could be causing your headache will also mean you know if you need medical help.

What Causes Frequent Headaches?

There are a number of reasons as to why people may suffer regular headaches. Let’s take a look at some of these causes below.

1. Stress and Mental Pressure

Stress can cause headaches. If you are very busy at work, stressed or facing problems in your life, you may tighten the muscles in your neck, shoulders and head and get a tension headache. These headaches tend to give a feeling of pressure around the front of your head.

2. Lack of Sleep

Lack of a regular sleep pattern can cause headaches. If you have poor sleep patterns, you may find it difficult to concentrate and you could feel tired. Overall it can affect our overall health.

3. Dehydration

Not drinking enough can lead to dehydration. If you are spending a lot of time outside, exercising or working in the heat, it is very easy to become dehydrated. Keeping hydrated within your daily routine is very important.

4. Excessive Screen Time

If you use computers, mobile phones and other electronic devices a lot then you may be getting headaches from eye strain and incorrect postures. To avoid this it is best to take breaks, change the screen brightness and sit comfortably.

5. Migraine

Migraine causes varying degrees of pain, often on one side of the head. Nausea, sensitivity to light and noise and visual disturbances are often present. Migraines can be very disabling and stop people working and carrying out daily tasks.

6. Skipping Meals and Dietary Habits

Some individuals develop a headache when they miss meals. Eating certain foods, having too much caffeine or an irregular eating pattern can cause headaches. To improve your health it is best to follow a healthy diet with regular meal times.

When Should We Seek Medical Advice?

If you have occasional headaches which are eased by rest, drinking water and making healthy lifestyle changes, you may be fine. You have a very severe headache all of a sudden, especially if you are weak, feel numb, are confused, have trouble talking, have blurred vision, a high temperature or stiff neck, and seek emergency medical help. If you had a blow to the head, you should also seek urgent medical assessment.

How Can We Reduce the Risk of Frequent Headaches?

Although we cannot prevent every headache, a few healthy habits may help reduce common triggers.

  • Drink enough water throughout the day.
  • Maintain a regular sleeping schedule.
  • Avoid skipping meals.
  • Take short breaks during extended screen use.
  • Practise relaxation techniques to manage stress.
  • Maintain good posture while working.
  • Keep a headache diary to identify possible triggers.

We should also avoid taking pain-relief medicines too frequently without medical advice, as overuse can contribute to recurring headaches.
