Eye Care and Ophthalmology Services Near Rolla, Sharjah

01-09-2026

Eyes are one of the most vital parts of our daily life. We depend on our eyes for almost everything we do, from working and reading to driving, using electronic devices, and having fun with our families. But eye health is often something we only consider when we have a problem.

At Metro Starcare Medical Center, we believe that taking care of our eyes should be an important part of our overall health care. If you’re having eye symptoms or changes in your vision, or if you simply want to be proactive about your eye health, our ophthalmology services are here to help with professional, personalized care.

Why is routine eye care important?

In some eye conditions, there are no obvious symptoms in the early stages. Sometimes you may not notice the vision changes because they happen gradually. Eye exams allow us to understand our eye health and any problems we might have that require professional help.

Our eye health and vision services specialize in diagnosing and treating a wide range of eye health conditions and eye diseases for all age groups. We want to be able to hear each patient’s specific concerns and have a plan for how we can help.

We also know that our lifestyle can have an impact on our eyes. Long hours in front of computers and mobile screens, working in demanding environments, or simply getting older can change our eye care needs. That is the message we want to convey to our patients: be aware of your vision and seek professional advice if you see something unusual.

When Should You See an Ophthalmologist?

We don’t have to wait for a vision problem to affect our daily routine to see a professional. There are some symptoms that might be a good reason to see an eye doctor:

  • Decreased or blurred vision
  • Eye redness or irritation that will not clear up
  • Sore, painful eyes
  • Too much water or not enough
  • Common eye strain
  • Cannot concentrate
  • Light sensitivity
  • Trouble seeing well at night
  • Noticeable changes in vision

Some symptoms can be caused by simple reasons, but if they persist or change suddenly, you should seek the advice of a qualified medical professional and not self-diagnose.
