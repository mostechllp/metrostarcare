How Diabetes and High Blood Pressure Affects Your Kidneys? UAE Patients Must Know

30-09-2026

The kidneys are essential organs and they perform various vital functions in the body. However, diseases like diabetes and high blood pressure can considerably damage the kidneys. To prevent such chronic illnesses, it is essential that you understand the correlation between these conditions and their impact on the kidneys.

How Does Diabetes Affect the Kidneys?

Diabetes occurs when there is a high blood sugar level in the body. If left untreated, diabetes can considerably damage the kidneys. The high sugar levels in the blood can damage the blood vessels in the kidneys.

There are minimal symptoms that show such a condition is present. There may be few or no symptoms indicating that such a condition is present. Therefore, it is essential that you get tested and examined by a medical practitioner regularly, especially if you are already suffering from diabetes. They would be able to detect any anomalies in the body and suggest the best possible treatment for the early stages of the disease.

Keeping track of your blood sugar levels can help in treating the condition and preventing further complications. You must also consume a healthy diet, stay physically fit and take the medicines as prescribed by the doctors.

How Does High Blood Pressure Affect the Kidneys?

High blood pressure is a condition where the blood pressure in the body is higher than normal. Chronic high blood pressure can cause extensive damage to the kidneys. It can deteriorate the small blood vessels in the kidneys and impact their overall function.

It is a two-way process where high blood pressure can affect the kidneys, as well as kidney diseases can also cause high blood pressure. Therefore, if you are suffering from either of these two conditions, it is essential that you get yourself tested regularly for kidney diseases. Controlling your blood pressure in accordance with your doctor’s guidelines is the most effective way to prevent chronic kidney diseases.

Why Should UAE Patients Be Concerned?

If you are a UAE citizen and are suffering from high blood pressure or diabetes, it is essential that you get yourself tested and examined by a medical practitioner regularly for kidney problems. You might not always have the opportunity to get yourself tested due to a busy schedule. However, it is important to stay on top of it and undergo regular tests. We advise that you have detailed discussions with your doctor about your risks for chronic kidney disease.

Depending on your age, medical history and overall health, you may need regular tests including blood pressure tests, blood tests and urine tests. Such tests would be helpful in determining if you are suffering from chronic kidney disease or not. Your doctor would prescribe an appropriate treatment plan in case they determine that you are suffering from one.

Diabetes and Kidney Health: What You Should Do?

Diabetes and Kidney Health can considerably impact the kidney health of a person, it is essential that you look after your overall health. We advise that you keep track of your blood pressure and blood sugar levels on a regular basis and get tested by a medical practitioner.

You must consume healthy meals and stay fit. In addition, you must also limit your salt consumption and refrain from smoking. Most importantly, you must consume the medicines as prescribed by the doctors. Do not make any changes to your diet or medication without the recommendation of your doctor.

At Metro Starcare, we believe that education plays a vital role in managing your health and we aim at providing our patients with reliable information regarding kidney diseases and how they can prevent them.

The best way to reduce the risk of chronic kidney disease is to have a healthy lifestyle and keep yourself healthy. In addition, you must get regular tests done and consult your doctor in case of any concern. If you are a UAE citizen living with diabetes or high blood pressure, it is essential that you take care of your kidney health to ensure a fit and healthy lifestyle.
