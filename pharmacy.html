Pharmacy Services at Metro Starcare

The right diagnosis is just as important as the right medicine at the right time. That’s why we have a fully stocked in-house pharmacy at Metro Starcare Medical Center, so you can collect your prescription right after your doctor – no extra stop, no running around town looking for a specific medicine.

Our pharmacy carries a wide range of prescription medications along with everyday over-the-counter essentials, so whether you've just walked out of a consultation or simply need something for a headache or a cold, we've got you covered. We have a good stock that is always topped up so you don't have to wait around or be told to call back.

Our pharmacists don’t just hand you a bag of medicine and walk away, they actually take the time to explain how to take what you’ve been prescribed, what to watch for, and answer any questions you might have about dosages or possible interactions with other medications you’re on. If you’re living with a long-term condition and need regular refills, our team will also make sure that process is easy and hassle-free.

Safety is something we don't compromise on. Every prescription is double-checked before it reaches you, and our pharmacists stay in close touch with your doctors here at Metro Starcare, so there's no confusion or mix-up along the way. We also make sure medicines are stored properly and kept well within their shelf life, because you deserve treatments that actually work the way they're supposed to.

You'll find our pharmacy right inside Metro Starcare Medical Centre, Rolla, Sharjah — easy to get to whether you're coming from a consultation upstairs or just stopping by on your own. Got a prescription to fill, or just need some advice on an over-the-counter medicine? Walk in, and our team will take care of you.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. The accuracy and safety of your prescriptions are checked on your behalf by us.

We maintain close collaboration between our Pharmacists and the physician. All medications are kept at the required temperature. Located within the same facility as our doctors, you can simply walk in and your prescriptions can be ready.

2. In addition to prescriptions, do you dispense over-the-counter medicines?

Yes. We fill prescriptions written by our physicians. We also fill over the counter products for common colds, headaches, etc.

3. Will the pharmacist discuss with me the proper use and potential side effects of my prescription?

Yes, your pharmacists will go through each prescription with you to explain how to use it, possible side effects and answer any questions you have.

4. Can the pharmacy manage my long-term medication refills?

Yes. We can provide regular refills of your maintenance medications to create a convenient refill schedule for you.

5. Are my medicines secure within your pharmacy?

Yes. All prescriptions are reviewed for accuracy and security. Our Pharmacists work directly with our physicians and medications are stored at appropriate temperatures, as required.
